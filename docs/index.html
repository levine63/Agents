Current Projects

Current work on lead exposure, treatment access, and frontline care.

Lead Exposure

Sentinel Firewall

Lead exposure costs the world trillions of dollars each year and causes more than one million deaths. I study whether distributing safer products to pregnant women and infants can be a cost-effective way to reduce exposure during the most vulnerable stages of life.

  • Working paper on safer products for pregnant women and infants
Policy brief (PDF)
Health Delivery

ORS

Diarrhea remains one of the leading causes of death among young children, even though ORS and zinc are inexpensive and effective treatments. We tested whether community health workers in Uganda could increase use by making treatment free, convenient, and available before a child falls ill.

  • The role of price and convenience in use of oral rehydration salts to treat child diarrhea in Uganda
Briefing paper
AI + Frontline Care

CHW Navigator

Community health workers are often the first line of care, but they rarely have consistent decision support. CHW Navigator explores whether AI-assisted tools can help them give clearer advice, follow up better, and improve care for families.

  • Large Language Models to Support Community Health Workers

Recent Projects

Recent interventions in schools, workplaces, and household energy.

Behavior Change

Handwashing

Handwashing with soap is one of the most effective ways to reduce diarrheal disease and respiratory infections, two of the largest causes of child illness and death. We tested a school-based intervention in southern India and found that combining low-cost soapy bottles with routines dramatically increased handwashing.

  • Rapid Prototyping a School-based Health Program in the Developing World
Hygiene Heroes handwashing page
Workplace Safety

OSHA

OSHA and other regulators inspect only a small fraction of the workplaces they oversee. Our work shows that better targeting, including machine learning, can help inspections prevent far more injuries without increasing the inspection budget.

  • Improving Regulatory Effectiveness Through Better Targeting: Evidence from OSHA
Policy essay
Energy and Health

Cookstoves

Smoke from biomass cooking causes a large burden of illness, especially for women and children. Our work tests how behavior change, logistics, and product design can help households move from smoky chulhas to cleaner fuels and sustain near-exclusive LPG use.

  • Incentivizing Elimination of Biomass Cooking Fuels with a Reversible Commitment and a Spare LPG Cylinder
Policy brief
