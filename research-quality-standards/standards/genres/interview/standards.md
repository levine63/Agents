## Scope

These standards apply to interview notes, interview summaries, transcripts, and qualitative field notes prepared by interns, RAs, or agents.

## Metadata

The submission should record:

- interviewer name
- interviewee name, role, or stable pseudonym
- how contact was established
- date and time
- language
- format or modality
- recording status

Missing recruitment or connection information is an `ERROR` at `DRAFT` and `SUBMISSION`.

## Consent And Access

The artifact should state:

- whether consent was obtained
- whether consent was written or verbal when relevant
- what the participant was told about use of the material

If the submission is an interview guide or field-ready instrument rather than post-hoc notes, it should also include an explicit consent script or a clear pointer to one.

Undocumented consent procedure is an `ERROR` at `DRAFT` and `SUBMISSION`.

## Contextual Sufficiency

A reader who was not in the room should be able to understand references, jargon, and topic shifts.

The artifact should:

- clarify ambiguous references
- define local jargon or acronyms
- preserve relevant contextual notes

When designing or reviewing interview guides, prefer open-ended questions before narrower probes when that sequencing materially reduces biasing the response.

Avoid leading prompts unless they are explicitly justified for a structured elicitation purpose.

If probes are used across respondents, they should be applied consistently enough to preserve comparability.

## Evidence Versus Interpretation

- direct quotes should be distinguishable from paraphrases
- interpretation should not be disguised as observation
- high-stakes claims should be tied to actual language from the interview where feasible

Quotes not distinguished from paraphrases are an `ERROR` at all stages beyond `EXPLORATORY`.

## Non-Verbal And Reliability Context

When relevant to interpretation, note:

- hesitation
- contradiction
- strong emotion
- unusual non-verbal cues

At `SUBMISSION`, the interviewer should also record brief reflexive concerns about reliability, completeness, or generalizability.

## Storage And Anonymization

The artifact should state:

- where raw notes or transcripts are stored
- whether anonymization was used
- how anonymization was performed when applicable

Missing anonymization procedure when anonymization is used is an `ERROR`.
