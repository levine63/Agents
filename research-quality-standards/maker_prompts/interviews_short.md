# Interview Maker Prompt

Produce interview notes or summaries that an outside reader can interpret.

Required:

- record interviewer, interviewee or pseudonym, connection source, date, modality, language, and recording status
- document consent and how the data may be used
- distinguish quotes from paraphrases
- explain jargon and ambiguous references
- note high-value non-verbal or reliability context when relevant
- list unresolved follow-up questions in `Not Yet Done`
