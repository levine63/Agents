## Good weekly goal

`Create a first draft of the attrition table for the baseline survey, using the approved respondent ID crosswalk, and save the Stata output and table draft in the project folder.`

## Weak weekly goal

`Work on attrition.`

## Checklist

A good weekly goal is:

- tied to a parent goal
- small enough for one week
- concrete enough to verify
- linked to an artifact
- clear about `done`
- honest about blockers
