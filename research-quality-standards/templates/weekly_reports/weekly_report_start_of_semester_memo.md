# Weekly Reporting Memo

Your weekly report helps the supervisor understand:

- what you produced
- what you will produce next
- what is blocking you

If your report is vague, it is hard to help you.
If it is clear, obstacles can be removed quickly.

## What counts as done

A task is done only if:

- it produces something someone else can open and use
- and you include a link or file showing it

Bad:

- `Worked on literature review`

Good:

- `Drafted 2-page summary of lead exposure literature plus table of 12 papers [link]`

## What carried over means

A task is carried over if:

- you listed it as planned last week
- and it is still not finished this week

Carried-over work is not a problem by itself.

Unexplained carried-over work is a problem.

## Submission details

- Use the exact format requested
- Reports missing links or clear output details may be returned

## What good reports look like

A good report:

- shows clear finished work with links
- explains unfinished work and why
- lists specific next outputs
- makes clear requests for help

A weak report:

- lists activities rather than outputs
- has no links or unclear outputs
- asks for help without context
