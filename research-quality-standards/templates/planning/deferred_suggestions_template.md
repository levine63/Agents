## Status guide

- `open` = suggested and still worth considering
- `deferred` = intentionally postponed
- `declined` = explicitly not wanted
- `adopted` = later implemented
- `superseded` = replaced by a better or newer suggestion
- `stale` = no longer worth attention

## Entry format

| Date | Suggestion | Why it mattered | Status | Revisit trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| YYYY-MM-DD | Short description | Risk, quality, or workflow reason | open / deferred / declined / adopted / superseded / stale | When to bring it up again |

## Notes

- Only record suggestions that are important enough to merit future review.
- Do not use this as a dumping ground for every optional idea.
- Keep this file project-specific.
- When an item becomes adopted, superseded, or stale, update its status rather than duplicating it.
- If a suggestion becomes high priority, move or summarize it in `Priority revisit`.
